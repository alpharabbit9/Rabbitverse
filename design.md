# Rabbit Verse Design System
**Version:** 2.0
**Project:** Rabbit Verse — Your Life Operating System

---

# Design Philosophy

Rabbit Verse is **not** a productivity app.

It is a **Life Operating System** that helps users understand, improve, and reflect on their lives.

The interface should feel like opening a calm, premium companion—not a spreadsheet full of tasks.

The product should make users feel:

- Calm
- Motivated
- Supported
- Inspired
- Emotionally connected

Every design decision should reinforce this philosophy.

---

# Core Design Principles

Rabbit Verse combines four major design pillars:

## 1. Premium SaaS

Inspired by:

- Linear
- Stripe
- Apple
- Arc Browser
- Vercel

Characteristics

- Minimal
- Spacious
- Elegant
- High-end
- Polished

---

## 2. Emotional Companion

The UI should never feel robotic.

Instead of saying

> Complete your tasks.

It should say

> You're building great momentum today.

Every interaction should feel supportive.

---

## 3. Storytelling

Rabbit Verse tells the story of your life.

The dashboard should feel like:

> "Here's what happened today."

instead of

> "Here's your database."

---

## 4. Calm Technology

Technology should fade into the background.

Information should be:

- simple
- beautiful
- emotionally meaningful

---

# Theme System

Rabbit Verse supports four adaptive layers.

1. Dark Theme (Default)
2. Light Theme
3. Dynamic Time Theme
4. Mood Theme

---

# 🌙 Dark Theme (Default Identity)

Dark Mode is the primary identity of Rabbit Verse.

Imagine opening the app at **10 PM** after work.

Instead of bright white screens…

You enter a calm digital space.

The feeling should resemble:

- Spotify
- Apple Music
- Arc Browser
- Apple TV

The interface should immediately reduce visual stress.
